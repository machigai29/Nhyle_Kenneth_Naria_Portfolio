Support Auto-Responder · 13 source nodes
  1. Receive a support ticket and return an acknowledgement.
  2. Normalize the ticket, then classify it and draft a reply.
  3. Send the draft to a person for approval.
  4. If approved, email the reply and log it as sent.
  5. Otherwise, log it as rejected or expired.

Approval gates: 1 · AI calls: 2

Video-Generator · 134 source nodes · 8 stages
  1. Receive and validate a Telegram request.
  2. Prepare the supplied text, link, image, or voice input.
  3. Gemini analyzes the source and drafts a storyboard.
  4. Pause for scene approval.
  5. Process approved scenes through the clip-generation loop.
  6. Prepare voiceover and assemble the video.
  7. Pause for final video approval.
  8. Route workflow failures to the error handler.

Approval gates: 2 · AI calls before the storyboard stop: 2

Workflow Watchdog · 6 source nodes
  1. Receive a workflow error event.
  2. Normalize the workflow, node, and error details.
  3. Ask Gemini to explain the failure.
  4. Build a concise alert.
  5. Send the alert through Telegram.

Approval gates: 0 · AI calls: 1
